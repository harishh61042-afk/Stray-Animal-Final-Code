From a street report to a forever home

Every step is recorded, so everyone involved can see what has happened and what comes next.

Report

Share a photo, the place and how urgent it is. Anyone can report, even without an account.

Rescue

An administrator reviews the report and assigns a verified rescuer, who accepts the case and brings the animal to safety.

Care

Veterinarians record examinations and vaccinations while a shelter gives the animal a safe place to recover.

Adopt

Healthy animals are listed for adoption. Adopters apply online and follow their application until it is complete.

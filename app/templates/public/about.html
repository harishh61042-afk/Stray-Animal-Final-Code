About PawConnect

One place for everyone who helps stray animals

PawConnect is a coordination platform for stray animal rescue, veterinary care, adoption and volunteering.

Why PawConnect exists

When someone sees an injured or abandoned animal, the help it needs is usually spread across many people: a rescuer who can reach the place, a veterinarian who can treat the animal, a shelter that has room, volunteers who can feed and transport, and finally a family that can adopt. Without a shared system these people rely on phone calls and messages, and details get lost.

PawConnect puts all of them on the same page. A report from the street turns into a tracked rescue case, the rescued animal gets a record with its medical history, and when the animal is healthy it can be listed for adoption.

What you can do here

  • Report an animal in need with photos, the location on a map and an urgency level, even without an account.
  • Coordinate rescues: administrators assign verified rescuers, who accept or decline and keep the case updated.
  • Keep records of every rescued animal, its examinations, treatments and vaccinations.
  • Manage shelters with capacity limits, so no shelter is overloaded by accident.
  • Adopt through a clear application process that shelters and administrators review.
  • Volunteer for tasks such as feeding, transport and shelter care once an administrator approves you.

How we keep it trustworthy

  • Rescuers, veterinarians and shelters are verified by an administrator before they can use their tools.
  • Volunteers must be approved before they can accept tasks.
  • A reporter's phone number and e-mail address are visible only to administrators and to the rescuer assigned to the case.
  • Every status change of a report or an animal is recorded with who made it and when.
  • A shelter cannot receive more animals than its capacity unless an administrator overrides the limit and writes down the reason.
  • Notifications appear inside PawConnect. The platform does not send e-mails or text messages.

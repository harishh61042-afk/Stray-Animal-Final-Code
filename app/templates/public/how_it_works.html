The process

How PawConnect works

Six steps take an animal from a street report to a new home. Everyone involved sees the same record.

  1. 1. Someone reports an animal

    Fill in the report form with the animal type, its condition, how urgent it is, a photo and the place. Pin the location on the map or use your device location. You get a report ID such as RPT-2026-00042.

    Anyone - no account needed

  2. 2. An administrator reviews and assigns

    The report is checked and a verified rescuer is assigned. The rescuer accepts or declines; if they decline, the administrator picks someone else.

    Administrators and rescuers

  3. 3. The rescue happens

    The rescuer keeps the case updated while the rescue is in progress. When the animal is safe, it is registered in PawConnect with its own record.

    Rescuers

  4. 4. The animal is cared for

    A shelter with free capacity takes the animal in. Veterinarians record examinations, treatments and vaccinations, and the animal's status follows its recovery.

    Veterinarians and shelters

  5. 5. Volunteers lend a hand

    Approved volunteers accept tasks such as feeding, transport, shelter cleaning and adoption events, and report their progress and hours.

    Volunteers

  6. 6. The animal is adopted

    When an animal is healthy it is listed for adoption. Adopters apply online, the shelter and administrators review the application, and an approved adoption is completed and recorded.

    Adopters, shelters and administrators
